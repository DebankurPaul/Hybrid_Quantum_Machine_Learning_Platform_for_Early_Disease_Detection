Empirical Evaluation Synthesis

On this standardized 8-dimensional PCA projection, quantum circuits demonstrate measurable sensitivity parity with classical models while exhibiting slight margins in accuracy (-1.8% to -3.5%) and markedly higher inference simulation latency. Results reflect algorithmic characteristics on compressed representations and do not constitute clinical deployment clearance.
